CoordBlock — datum / projection / altitude readouts in mono with signal-colored keys.

```jsx
<CoordBlock direction="column" items={[{key:"Datum",value:"WGS 84"},{key:"Lat",value:"40.7128 N"},{key:"Proj",value:"UTM 18N"}]} />
```

Place bottom-left of heroes and HUDs; pair with a UnitId bottom-right.
